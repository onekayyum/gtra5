## Brand & Style

This design system defines the visual and interaction standard for the Grand Theft Auto VI official portal and cloud streaming platform. It balances intense cinematic adrenaline with ultra-responsive, technical utility required for direct low-latency gaming.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Cinematic Noir Meets Vice City Neon:** The interface emerges from a deeply atmospheric obsidian canvas, punctuated by hot sunset gradients, radiant magenta outlines, and electric cyan telemetry signals. 
- **Glassmorphism & Tactical HUD:** Panels utilize heavy backdrop blurs, subtle high-index frosted glass, and hair-thin gradient borders that evoke both coastal luxury and high-tech telemetry.
- **Tone & Mood:** Dangerous, glamorous, urgent, and hyper-premium. The interface recedes into absolute darkness to frame 4K cinematic streaming media and game art while providing immediate tactical clarity for cloud launch actions.

### Strict Nomenclature Guidelines
- Approved titles are strictly limited to **Grand Theft Auto VI** or **GTA 6**. No informal abbreviations, colloquialisms, or unofficial tags are permitted in interface labels or telemetry strings.

## Colors

The palette directly reflects the heat, dusk, and underworld glamour of Vice City.

### Color Tokens & Roles
- **Primary (`#e0247d`):** Electric Vice Magenta. Used for key interactive triggers, active tab indicators, neon outer glows, and critical game status accents.
- **Secondary (`#f97316`):** Sunset Tangerine. Blended directly with magenta to produce the signature sunset gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, #f97316 0%, #e0247d 50%, #7928ca 100%)`). Applied to primary "PLAY NOW" call-to-actions, VIP status pills, and special edition highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#06b6d4`):** Electric Ocean Cyan. Used for high-octane gaming telemetry: ping readouts, server latency badges, cloud bitrates, and verified co-op squad notifications.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#0a0a0f`):** Obsidian Black. True-depth dark background optimizing contrast ratio for OLED displays and cinematic artwork.
- **Surface Elevation Cards (`#12121a`):** Deep Slate. Baseline background for cards, flyout drawers, floating navigation docks, and dialog modals.
- **Crisp Accents (`#ffffff`):** Pure optical white reserved for high-contrast titles, primary icons, and active input text. Muted typography defaults to `#94a3b8` and `#64748b`.

### Gradient Implementations
- **Vice Sunset Glow:** `linear-gradient(90deg, #f97316, #ec4899, #e0247d)`
- **Glass Rim Highlight:** `linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15) 0%, rgba(224, 36, 125, 0.2) 50%, rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.05) 100%)`

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the high-velocity, condensed punch of classic arcade and sports telemetry with modern readability.

- **Headlines & Action Hero Display (`Barlow Condensed`):** Rendered in uppercase with slight tracking to evoke high-speed sports broadcasting and cinematic film credits.
- **Narrative & System Interface (`Chivo`):** Modern, sharp, and razor-precise. It provides exceptional legibility against dark translucent glass plates.
- **Telemetry & Technical HUD (`JetBrains Mono`):** Dedicated to technical readouts, cloud server regions, latency graphs, FPS counters, and session IDs.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout utilizes a high-density, media-first 12-column grid designed for 4K TVs, ultrawide gaming monitors, and handheld/mobile interfaces.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column layout with `3rem` canvas margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Content containers span a maximum width of `1600px` for ultra-dense multimedia immersion.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters. HUD docks collapse into bottom navigation modules.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4-column layout with `1.25rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Media cards stack vertically or switch to horizontal touch carousels.

### Layout Principles
- **Overlapping Viewports:** Key character assets and vehicle cutouts should break out of their layout containers, resting in elevated visual layers above section dividers.
- **Docked Cloud HUD:** Floating bottom and side controls float consistently `1.5rem` above the canvas floor, anchored through fixed glass viewports.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on traditional drop shadows. Instead, it employs atmospheric glass depth, neon photoluminescence, and high-frequency edge strokes.

### Elevation Levels

- **Base Layer (Elevation 0):** Pure `#0a0a0f` deep obsidian tone. May feature high-contrast background wallpaper, film grain, or muted ambient radial glows.
- **HUD Plates & Rail (Elevation 1):** Background `#12121a` at 85% opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`. Border is a 1px solid stroke with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Raised Focus Cards (Elevation 2):** `#181824` with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`. Border uses top/left gradient stroke (`rgba(224, 36, 125, 0.35)` blending into `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`).
- **Active Interactive Elements (Elevation 3):** Highlighted with dual-state radial photoluminescence:
  - *Magenta Bloom:* `box-shadow: 0 0 25px -4px rgba(224, 36, 125, 0.5), 0 0 10px -2px rgba(249, 115, 22, 0.3)`
  - *Cyan Telemetry Glow:* `box-shadow: 0 0 20px -3px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.6)`
- **Modals & Video Drawers (Elevation 4):** Deep slate `#0e0e17` backed by an 80% opacity dark vignette backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(32px)`).

## Shapes

The design system incorporates a razor-sharp, precision-cut industrial aesthetic. Large radius curves conflict with aggressive gaming typography; therefore, geometry remains controlled, architectural, and compact.

- **Base Corners (`0.25rem` / `4px`):** Standard for buttons, input fields, badge pills, and system sliders.
- **Container Surfaces (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** Used for game cards, media containers, and modal dialogs.
- **Chamfered Cuts:** Specific primary actions and HUD telemetry panels allow for a diagonal `6px` clip-path cut on top-right and bottom-left corners to amplify high-tech styling.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary ("Launch Direct Play"):** Sunset gradient fill (`#f97316` to `#e0247d`). Typography is `Barlow Condensed` Bold, all caps with `0.1em` letter spacing. Features a continuous, slow ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(224, 36, 125, 0.4)`). On hover, brightness escalates to 110% with a `scale(1.02)` transform.
- **Secondary ("Trailer / Details"):** Translucent obsidian slate (`rgba(18, 18, 26, 0.8)`), 1px solid stroke in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`. On hover, the border turns electric cyan (`#06b6d4`) with a matching subtle inner glow.
- **Tertiary ("System / Settings"):** Borderless, muted white typography (`#94a3b8`) transitioning to pure white with a bottom 2px magenta indicator upon focus.

### Chips & Badges
- **Platform Telemetry Badges:** Background `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.1)` with 1px border in `#06b6d4`. Typography is `JetBrains Mono` 11px uppercase in cyan (`4K 60FPS`, `HDR10`, `CLOUD ACTIVE`).
- **Vice City Status Badges:** Sunset gradient outline with a dark obsidian core. Pill-shaped or chamfered, containing an animated pulsing dot indicator.

### Cards (Media & Character Selectors)
- **Cinematic Feature Card:** 16:9 or 21:9 aspect ratio with an edge-to-edge high-resolution character image. Overlaid with an internal bottom-to-top obsidian vignette gradient (`rgba(10, 10, 15, 0)` to `#0a0a0f`). Bottom area holds `Barlow Condensed` titles and quick-action overlay triggers.
- **Hover State:** Card scales smoothly (`1.015`), border transitions from muted slate to an electric magenta glow, and video teaser plays automatically.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Search & Filter Bars:** Slender height (44px) filled with dark slate (`#12121a`), bounded by a 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`. Focus state produces an instant `#06b6d4` cyan rim light.
- **Sliders & Toggles:** Toggles utilize obsidian tracks with sunset gradient pills that emit a magenta bloom when engaged.

### Direct Play Cloud Telemetry HUD
- A dedicated micro-dock displaying real-time stream stability: ping indicator colored conditionally (Cyan `< 20ms`, Orange `20-50ms`, Magenta `> 50ms`), audio bitrate, and cloud datacenter region tag.
